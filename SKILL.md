# Personalized Application Writer

Tailor an application to the real evidence in your experience while preserving a natural, truthful voice.

## When to use

You are applying for an opportunity and need relevant, specific writing.

## Workflow

1. Read the prompt and selection criteria closely.
2. Build an evidence map from supplied experience.
3. Draft around concrete choices and outcomes.
4. Check every claim and keep the applicant’s voice.

## Output

Return a usable result for the user’s actual task. State assumptions, evidence, and uncertainty where they affect the result. Keep the output format suited to the job: working HTML, a storyboard, a decision brief, a draft, code, or a short answer. Do not claim that a result was tested, published, or verified unless that action was completed.

## Starter prompt

> Help me write [application type] for [opportunity]. Use only the experiences I provide. First map each selection criterion to evidence, and mark gaps instead of inventing them. Ask questions where a concrete detail would improve the draft. Then draft in my voice: [voice notes], within [word limit]. Use specific actions and outcomes, avoid generic claims, and keep the strongest detail. Finish with a fact-check list of every claim I should confirm.

## Quality check

Before delivery, check that the result answers the stated need, follows the requested format, and makes any important limitation visible. Invite the user to adjust the result when taste or judgment belongs to them.
